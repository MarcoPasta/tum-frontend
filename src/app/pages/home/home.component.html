!TUM is cancelled.

It is with a heavy heart that we need to announce that !TUM 2024 cannot happen after all.

Even after several inspections and planning meetings with the location before the announcement, we have now found several showstoppers that wouldn’t allow us to party with you the way we all expect.

A big thank you to all the organizers and helpers that stepped up to make !TUM happen, the support by the TUM-organizers, and the many excited and kind messages from everyone planning to visit the party.

We will see each other at one of the next upcoming demoparties, and hope that we can pull things off in a future edition of !TUM.
